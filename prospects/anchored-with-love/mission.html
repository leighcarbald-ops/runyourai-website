What anchors us

Fueled by love.
Focused on the future.

Building Better Kids. Bridging Families. Creating Better Communities.

Anchored with Love Inc empowers underserved youth ages 10–17 through hands-on learning, mentoring and life preparation. Our faith-based approach connects character and confidence with useful skills.

We believe this work reaches beyond one young person. Families and community partners are part of building a stronger foundation, together.

Founded in 2025, our 501(c)(3) nonprofit serves Metro Atlanta and Columbus, Georgia.
