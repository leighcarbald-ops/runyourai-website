Privacy Policy

Anchored with Love Inc respects your privacy.

We may collect information you voluntarily provide through contact forms, donations, registrations, and inquiries, including your name, email, phone number, and donation details.

We use this information to respond to inquiries, administer programs, process donations, communicate updates, and fulfill legal obligations.

Payment information is handled through our payment providers.

We do not sell personal information.

We may share necessary information with trusted service providers or when required by law.

We take reasonable steps to protect information but cannot guarantee absolute security.

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies through third-party services.

Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

To request access, correction, or deletion of your information, contact anchoredwithloveinc@gmail.com, subject to legal recordkeeping requirements.

As stated in our website review responses, information is retained for three years. Submit a formal written request for access, correction or deletion to anchoredwithloveinc@gmail.com, subject to legal recordkeeping requirements. Access is limited to the Executive Director and authorized individuals or community partners who need it for their responsibilities.

This mock website prepares email drafts locally; it has no form submission backend, analytics or payment collection. Email providers and the hosting provider may process information separately.

Youth Privacy & Safety

We are committed to protecting the privacy and safety of the youth we serve.

We collect only information reasonably necessary for program registration, participation, safety, and communication with authorized parents or guardians.

Parents or legal guardians must provide required permissions for minors to participate and for any applicable collection or use of their information.

We do not knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13 without legally required parental consent.

We do not publicly disclose a child's home address, personal contact information, school, or other sensitive details without appropriate authorization.

Youth information is shared only as necessary for program operations, safety, service providers, or legal obligations.

Parents or guardians may contact us to ask questions, review applicable information, or request changes or deletion, subject to legal requirements.

This website inquiry form is for adults, parents and guardians. Do not include a child’s name, age, school, home address, contact information, health details or other sensitive information. Enrollment and consent must be handled separately through an authorized process.

Photo, Video & Media Consent

Anchored with Love Inc may photograph or record program activities for educational, promotional, fundraising, and community-awareness purposes.

Written permission from a parent or legal guardian is required before using identifiable photos, videos, or testimonials of minors.

Youth assent will also be sought when appropriate.

Approved media may appear on our website, social media, newsletters, publications, and promotional materials.

Participation in photography is voluntary and is not a condition of receiving services.

Consent may be withdrawn for future use by contacting anchoredwithloveinc@gmail.com.

Withdrawal cannot guarantee removal of materials already printed, distributed, or shared by third parties.

Photo and media consent must be obtained separately from this website policy.

No identifiable youth photographs, videos or testimonials are included in this mock website. Approved media and written permissions have not yet been supplied.

Donation Terms

Donations support the charitable mission and programs of Anchored with Love Inc.

Contributions may fund youth education, life skills, financial literacy, trades, technology, program supplies, and community initiatives.

Donations are processed through third-party payment providers and are subject to their terms and privacy practices.

Donors should review their contribution details before submitting payment.

Donations are generally voluntary and nonrefundable, except where required by law or approved by Anchored with Love Inc.

Contact anchoredwithloveinc@gmail.com promptly regarding duplicate, unauthorized, or mistaken transactions.

Donations to Anchored with Love Inc may be tax-deductible to the extent permitted by law.

Donors should retain receipts and consult a tax professional regarding their individual circumstances.

Donations designated for a particular purpose will be used consistently with applicable restrictions.

If a designated purpose becomes impractical or cannot be fulfilled, we will handle the funds in accordance with donor restrictions and applicable law.

This mock website collects no payments. Contact the organization with giving questions.
