About Lamb's Cakes

Small-bakery care. Big celebration energy.

Lamb's Cakes is a home-based bakery in Jennings, Louisiana, making homemade cakes and sweets to order—from simple favorites to specialty custom work.

Built around your order

Made personally, not mass produced.

The fun of a made-to-order bakery is that the order starts with the person, the party and the idea. Lamb's Cakes can work from a theme, color palette or sweet craving to create something that feels like it belongs at your celebration.
