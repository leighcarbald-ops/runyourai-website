About Lamb's Cakes

Small bakery care. Big celebration energy.

Lamb's Cakes is a small home-based bakery in Jennings, Louisiana making homemade cakes and sweet treats to order — from simple favorites to specialty custom work.

What makes it different?

The order begins with your occasion and idea. That gives each cake or treat room to feel personal instead of mass-produced.

Made personally

Not pulled from a shelf. Made for your table.

A home bakery can be more flexible and personal than a one-size-fits-all dessert counter. Lamb's Cakes works from the celebration, the look, and the kind of sweet you have in mind.

That can mean a themed birthday cake, cupcakes for a party, decorated cookies, a milestone dessert, chocolate-covered treats, pies, or a custom idea that starts with an inspiration picture.
