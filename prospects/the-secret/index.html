Welcome to The Secret

A private, adults-only social retreat created around connection, acceptance and simply being.

So… what exactly is The Secret?

Maybe something that doesn't have a name yet.

Part private social club. Part backyard retreat. Part community gathering place. The Secret started with one simple thought: what if there was a place where people could just… be?

Come as you are. Laugh too loud. Meet somebody you never would have met otherwise. Make something, wander outside, have a good conversation—or do absolutely nothing for a while.
