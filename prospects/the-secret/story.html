Our Story

It's the kind of place we wished existed.

So we're creating it.

It started with a pretty simple thought.

What if there was a place where people could just… be?

Come as you are. Sit around a fire. Laugh too loud. Meet somebody you never would have met otherwise. Play a game, listen to music, make something, wander outside, have a good conversation, or do absolutely nothing for a while.

Underneath all of it is something that matters deeply to us: people deserve places where they feel welcome.

The culture we're building

Welcome requires intention.

We're creating The Secret around connection, acceptance, respect, consent, and personal responsibility. The goal isn't sameness. It's making room for people to share a space well.

Come as you are.

You shouldn't have to perform belonging.

Respect the room.

Freedom works when it lives alongside consent and responsibility.

Leave room for people.

Different lives and different perspectives can still share laughter, conversation and community.

There will be fun. There will probably be shenanigans. There will definitely be laughter.

The story is only beginning.
