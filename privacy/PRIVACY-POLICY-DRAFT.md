# Proposed privacy policy revision — REVIEW ONLY

**Status:** Draft; not published. The live policy remains unchanged. Do not state that automatic deletion or specific retention periods are operational until deployed and verified.

## Suggested replacement: Data retention
> We retain information as needed to provide services, respond to requests, maintain security, comply with legal requirements, and resolve disputes. The appropriate period depends on the service, whether a request or case remains active, customer instructions, and applicable obligations. We are developing automated retention controls, but these controls are not yet active in production. Proposed defaults under review include 30 days for closed support conversations and 90 days for resolved tickets, knowledge-gap records, and certain visibility-audit records. These are **proposals, not current deletion guarantees**. Active or escalated support cases, pending appointments, paid or contractually retained reports, security records, and information subject to legal holds may need longer retention. Financial and transaction records may be retained for legally required periods.

## Suggested addition: Infrastructure and service providers
> Our services may use Cloudflare infrastructure, including Workers and D1 databases, to process website requests, support conversations, service records, and visibility audits. We may use email delivery providers such as Brevo for service notifications, and payment providers such as Stripe when payments are enabled. AI features may involve third-party model providers when specifically configured and enabled. The precise providers and processing depend on the service used.

## Suggested addition: Privacy requests
> You may email Help@runyourai.pro to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information. We may need to verify your identity and authority before acting. Requests may be limited by legal obligations, security, contractual requirements, and the rights of other people. If a business customer controls the relevant support information, we may direct your request to that business or coordinate with it.

## Publication checklist
- Confirm actual vendor/subprocessor usage and configuration (including whether Stripe is enabled).
- Confirm whether RunYourAI acts as controller or processor for each service and who handles end-user requests.
- Confirm current retention behavior and distinguish planned settings from actual guarantees.
- Review data categories and external disclosures, and obtain legal review as appropriate.
- Only after approval, update `privacy.html` with the verified wording and current revision date.

This is a technical draft, not legal advice.
