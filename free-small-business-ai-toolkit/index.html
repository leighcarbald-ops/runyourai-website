ChatGPT Slash Command Library

A library of quick prompt shortcuts for telling ChatGPT what kind of answer you want. It includes official or historical UI commands where documented and clearly labels user-defined shortcuts so you know the difference.

/truthAsk for a candid, evidence-focused assessment that separates facts, assumptions, risks and uncertainty.
/reasonA documented historical macOS slash-menu reasoning option, clearly marked because current model and UI names have changed.

PRACTICAL AI GUARDRAILS

Small Business AI Safety, Privacy & Accuracy Checklist

A practical checklist for everyday business AI use. It identifies information that deserves caution, information you should not enter into general AI chats, and situations where human review matters.

Customer personal informationUse only what is necessary and avoid sensitive customer data unless the tool and business use are approved for it.
Passwords & login credentialsNever paste passwords, recovery codes, private keys or authentication secrets into prompts.
Payment-card dataDo not use general AI chat as a place to process or store card numbers or CVV data.
