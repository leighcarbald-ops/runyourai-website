# RunYourAI Website North Star & QC Standard

Status: Required for customer websites and prospect demos.

North Star: Business-specific content + conversion architecture + strong technical QC + premium visual polish.

## Core rule
A RunYourAI website is not complete because it looks good or because all pages exist. It must feel specific to the business, guide visitors toward the business's primary conversion, work correctly across devices, and meet a premium visual standard.

All four gates must pass. One weak gate means the site is not finished.

## Gate 1 - Business Fit
- Copy sounds like this business, not a reusable template.
- Services, pricing, service area, hours, contact details and claims are verified for real clients.
- Design fits the category, audience, brand and price position.
- Imagery is relevant, intentional and legally usable.
- Home-based businesses do not expose residential addresses without explicit owner approval.
- The site answers: What do they do? Who is it for? Why choose them? What should I do next?
- Never invent reviews, awards, experience, certifications, staff, prices or business facts for a real client.
- Fictional demo content must not be confused with a real business.

## Gate 2 - Conversion Architecture
Define one primary conversion before design begins. Examples: salon = appointment; contractor = estimate; bakery = order; professional service = consultation.

Every page supports that conversion without becoming pushy.
- Hero communicates the value proposition quickly.
- Primary CTA appears early.
- Secondary CTA helps visitors not ready to convert.
- Services connect naturally to the next action.
- Trust appears before major conversion asks.
- Pricing or pricing context appears when appropriate.
- Copy reduces relevant objections and friction.
- Contact information is easy to find.
- Important pages end with a useful CTA.
- Forms request only what is needed for the next step.
- Mobile visitors can act without hunting.

## Gate 3 - Technical QC
Navigation/functionality:
- All navigation, logo, buttons and CTAs work.
- Forms work or are clearly labeled as non-submitting demos.
- Phone, email, map and social links are correct.
- No dead links, production placeholder links, broken images or references to another customer.

Responsive/mobile:
- Test phone, tablet and desktop.
- No horizontal overflow, clipping or collisions.
- Navigation, forms and buttons remain usable.
- Images crop intentionally and grids stack logically.
- Important CTA remains easy to reach.

Accessibility:
- Semantic heading order and meaningful alt text.
- Form labels and visible keyboard focus.
- Readable contrast.
- Important interaction does not depend only on hover.
- Motion remains restrained.

Performance:
- Images are appropriately sized/compressed.
- Avoid unnecessary libraries, effects and scripts.
- Prevent avoidable layout shift.
- No visual effect is worth a materially worse user experience.

SEO/fundamentals:
- Unique page titles and useful meta descriptions.
- One clear page topic/H1.
- Natural business/service/location language where relevant.
- Semantic HTML.
- Consider LocalBusiness structured data and social metadata for real launches.
- Check indexing/canonical/sitemap/robots behavior when applicable.

Accuracy/security/privacy:
- Verify real-business facts.
- No exposed secrets/tokens.
- Forms and analytics follow the chosen privacy approach.
- Use live open/closed status only when hours/timezone logic is trustworthy.

## Gate 4 - Premium Visual Polish
Premium does not mean adding every trendy effect.
- Strong typography hierarchy and consistent spacing rhythm.
- Intentional color system and high-quality relevant imagery.
- Clear visual hierarchy and consistent components.
- Thoughtful whitespace.
- Desktop and mobile both feel designed.
- Motion/micro-interactions are subtle and purposeful.
- Gradients, glass, glows, particles and decorative animation are optional tools, never defaults.
- Visual language changes with the business rather than making every RunYourAI site identical.

## Standard four-page build
1. Home: positioning, primary CTA, key services/offers, trust, differentiation, closing CTA.
2. About: real story/philosophy/people/credibility and reasons to choose the business.
3. Services: scannable service information, appropriate price context, decision support, contextual CTA.
4. Contact/Book/Quote: friction-light conversion page with contact methods, hours/service area/location as appropriate, form/booking path and reassurance.

Page names may change when the business needs better information architecture. Four pages is product scope, not permission to force the wrong structure.

## Demo standard
Prospect demos meet the same visual and conversion standard as paid sites. A demo is a portfolio piece and sales asset, not a rough mockup.
- Make the business/category feel specific.
- Demonstrate the likely customer journey.
- Use realistic but clearly fictional details when not based on a real prospect.
- Never lower design quality because it is only a demo.
- A prospect should understand the quality RunYourAI can build for them.

## Final pre-launch review
1. Business-fit pass: remove generic/template language and verify facts.
2. Conversion pass: trace the visitor journey from landing to action.
3. Content pass: spelling, grammar, consistency, pricing, hours, names and contact details.
4. Visual pass: hierarchy, spacing, imagery, typography and component consistency.
5. Mobile pass: every page at phone width.
6. Functional pass: click every nav item, CTA, contact method, form and external link.
7. Technical pass: accessibility basics, SEO metadata, performance, errors, security/privacy.
8. Cross-page uniformity pass: header/footer, CTA language, pricing presentation, navigation and visual system.
9. Conversion re-test: can a first-time visitor understand the offer and act quickly?
10. Final polish pass: fix anything that feels templated, unfinished, confusing or cheap.

## Stop-ship failures
Do not publish or hand off as complete with:
- Broken navigation, CTA, form or important link.
- Incorrect client facts or pricing.
- Obvious mobile layout failure.
- Generic copy that could belong to any competitor.
- Missing primary conversion path.
- Major readability/accessibility blocker.
- Placeholder content accidentally presented as real.
- Visibly inconsistent pages/components.
- Broken/missing imagery.
- A page materially less polished than the rest.

## Reference benchmark
Veloura Beauty Studio establishes the direction: visual quality and conversion strategy work together. Future sites should not copy its salon aesthetic. They should match its level of intentionality while being designed for their own business and conversion goal.
